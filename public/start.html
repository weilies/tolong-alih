How it works

Two drivers, one app. Here is what each of them sees.

1
If you double-park

Tell them before you walk away

Type the plate of the car you're blocking and how long you'll be. That's it.

2
If your car is blocked

Your phone tells you who, and when they're back

Tap Come now if you need to leave, or I'm waiting. You can also send a message.

3
When you drive off

Tap "I've moved"

The other driver is told they're free to go.

  • Neither driver ever sees the other's name or email.
  • Blocked but got no alert? Use Trace: type their plate and yours, and we'll find them.
  • Free for drivers.

Set up in 3 minutes

Do this once, now — not when you're already stuck in a car park.

Step 1 · Put Tolong Alih on your home screen

So alerts reach you even when your phone is locked. On iPhone, alerts only work after this step.

✓ Already installed — you're using it from your home screen.
Use Safari. If you opened this link from WhatsApp or Facebook, tap ⋯ and choose Open in Safari first.
  1. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow ⬆) at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. Then open Tolong Alih from its new icon.
Use Chrome. If you opened this link from WhatsApp or Facebook, tap ⋮ and choose Open in Chrome first.
  1. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right of Chrome.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app).
  3. Tap Install or Add. Then open Tolong Alih from its new icon.

Step 2 · Create your account

Use Google, or your email and a password. That is all we ask for. The other driver never sees your name or email.

Step 3 · Add your car plate

Tap Garage at the bottom of the app, type your plate, tap Add to garage. This is how you get an alert when someone blocks you.

Done. Your plate shows in your garage:

Step 4 · Allow alerts

The app asks once. Tap Allow alerts, then Allow.
