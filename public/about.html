Who we are

Next Novas is a small Malaysian studio building practical tools for everyday problems here. Tolong Alih is one of them.

Your data

We hold your email, phone number and the plates you register. Your number exists only so a driver can be reached when the app cannot reach them, and is never shown to another user. Location confirms you are in Malaysia and picks nearby offers; coordinates are never shown to other drivers.

Ask and we will export everything we hold on you, or delete your account outright.

How the four words work

  • Declare — you tell us you have blocked someone in.
  • Clear — you have moved. No confirmation needed; they may be streets away.
  • Flag — they said they moved and they have not.
  • Trace — you are blocked in but not registered. Enter both plates to find them.
