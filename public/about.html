Double parking, sorted

Tolong Alih connects two drivers by plate number when one blocks the other in. The blocker declares the block, the blocked driver is notified instantly, and the block is cleared when the car moves. Neither driver ever sees the other's phone number — everything routes through the platform.

Built and run in Malaysia by Next Novas, a small studio shipping practical tools for everyday Malaysian problems.

Plates can only be claimed once. If yours is taken, it is almost always the previous owner who never removed the car after selling it.

Privacy

What we hold

Your email, phone number and the plates you register. Your phone number exists only so a driver can be reached when the app cannot reach them, and is never shown to another user. Location is checked to confirm you are in Malaysia and to pick nearby offers; coordinates are never shown to other drivers.
